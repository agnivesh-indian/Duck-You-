Duck You!

You can't get rid of the ducks. You can only change how you row.

📖 What this is
Duck You! is a playful training ground for Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) — one of the most researched modern psychotherapies for anxiety, depression, and chronic worry.
🎯 How it works
1
Ducks = thoughts. They swim into the pond on their own. You don't control when they appear.
2
Tap a duck gently, 2–3 times. It turns and swims calmly across the pond, drifting out the far side.
!
Don't fight. Rapid, aggressive taps stir up the water — the duck stays longer and the pond loses its calm.
Research-backed Based on ACT's "Ducks on the Pond" metaphor and the Acceptance and Action Questionnaire (AAQ-II) construct of psychological flexibility. Studies show that practicing acceptance and defusion — observing thoughts without fighting them — predicts long-term mental health better than thought control.

The Ducks on the Pond

An ACT metaphor for a busy mind

💭 The metaphor
Imagine your mind is a pond. Thoughts are ducks. They surface on their own — you did not invite them, and you cannot stop them from appearing. You can only choose how you respond. Fighting them stirs the water and brings more. Letting them drift, without grasping, keeps the pond clear.
🔬 The science
Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) is an evidence-based approach developed by Steven Hayes and colleagues. Dozens of randomized controlled trials show ACT is effective for general anxiety, depression, chronic pain, and stress. The core mechanism is psychological flexibility — the ability to stay present, open, and engaged with what matters, even when difficult thoughts arise. Hayes, S. C., et al. (2006). Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: Model, processes, and outcomes. Behaviour Research and Therapy, 44(1), 1–25. · Twohig, M. P. & Levin, M. E. (2017). ACT in Practice.
🎯 What this tool teaches
🧘
Defusion — thoughts are events in the mind, not commands you must obey.
🌊
Acceptance — trying to eliminate thoughts gives them more power. Nudging them gently lets them fade.
🎯
Present moment — watching the pond keeps you here, now, not in the past or future.
Not a substitute for care. Duck You! is a self-guided practice tool inspired by ACT principles. If intrusive thoughts or anxiety are persistent, please reach out to a licensed mental health professional.

About this tool
Duck You! is a self-guided ACT practice tool based on the "Ducks on the Pond" metaphor — one of the core exercises in Acceptance and Commitment Therapy. The mechanic rewards slow, deliberate nudges and penalizes aggressive fighting, mirroring the ACT principle that acceptance dissolves difficult thoughts more effectively than control. Hayes, S. C., Strosahl, K. D., & Wilson, K. G. (2012). Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: The Process and Practice of Mindful Change (2nd ed.).
